# Returns and Refunds

## Can I return a product?

Because our products are fresh food, we cannot accept returns of opened or unopened items, and we cannot resell them. Instead, if something is wrong with your order, we make it right with a replacement or a refund.

## Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

If your order arrives damaged, if you received the wrong item, or if an item is missing:

1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery or pickup.
2. Send a photo of the damaged or incorrect item and your order number to hello@hearthandcrumb.example.
3. We reply within one business day and offer you a free replacement or a full refund for the affected items. You choose which one.

You do not need to send the product back to us. Claims made more than 48 hours after delivery cannot be accepted, because we cannot confirm the condition of fresh food after that time.

## Quality concerns

If a product does not meet your expectations (for example, a loaf is underbaked or stale on the day of purchase), bring it back to the shop on the same day or the next day with your receipt. We will replace it or give you store credit.

## Late or lost packages

If your package has not arrived 5 business days after it shipped, contact us. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a replacement or a full refund, including the shipping cost. We cannot refund packages that are delayed or spoiled because the shipping address was incorrect.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement. Purchases paid with cash are refunded in cash at the shop. Purchases paid with a gift card are refunded to the gift card.

## Custom cakes

Refunds and cancellations for custom cakes follow a separate deposit policy. See our custom orders information for details.
