# Shipping and Local Delivery

## Where we ship

We ship to all addresses in the contiguous United States (the lower 48 states). We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, P.O. boxes, or international addresses, because our products cannot stay fresh for the longer transit time.

## What we ship

Only products that stay fresh for several days can be shipped:

- Sourdough loaves and rye bread
- Cookies and biscotti
- Chocolate babka
- House granola

Croissants, cream-filled pastries, fruit tarts, and custom cakes cannot be shipped. These are available for in-store pickup or local delivery only.

## Shipping days and transit time

We ship on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday only, so packages do not sit in a warehouse over the weekend. All orders ship with 2-day delivery.

- Orders placed before 11:00 AM Pacific Time on a shipping day leave the same day.
- Orders placed after 11:00 AM, or on Thursday to Sunday, leave on the next shipping day.

You receive an email with a tracking number when your order ships.

## Shipping costs

- Orders under $75: flat rate of $12.
- Orders of $75 or more: free shipping.

Shipping costs are calculated before any discounts or gift cards are applied.

## Local delivery in Portland

We deliver within 10 miles of the shop, Tuesday to Saturday, between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM. Local delivery costs $8 per order, and there is no minimum order. Local delivery orders must be placed by 5:00 PM the day before. Local delivery is the only way to get croissants, pastries, and custom cakes delivered.

## Incorrect addresses

Please check your shipping address before you place your order. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we can reship it for the cost of shipping, if the products are still fresh enough to send.
