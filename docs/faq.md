# Frequently Asked Questions

Hearth & Crumb Bakery is a small, family-run bakery in Portland, Oregon. We bake bread, pastries, cookies, and custom cakes from scratch every morning.

## What payment methods do you accept?

In the shop, we accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Online orders accept the same cards plus Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept personal checks.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available in the shop and online in any amount from $10 to $200. Gift cards never expire and can be used for in-store purchases, online orders, and custom cakes.

## Do you have a loyalty program?

Yes. The Crumb Club is free to join at the register or on our website. You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend, and every 100 points gives you $10 off a future purchase. Points expire 12 months after you earn them.

## Where does your flour come from?

All of our wheat and rye flour is certified organic and comes from Cascade Valley Mill, a small mill about 60 miles south of Portland. Our butter, eggs, and milk come from farms in Oregon and Washington.

## How fresh is the bread?

Everything on the shelves is baked the same morning. Bread that is still left at 3:00 PM is sold at 50% off as "day's end" bread until closing. We donate any remaining bread to a local food bank.

## Do you sell to cafes and restaurants?

Yes. We supply bread and pastries to a small number of local businesses. Wholesale customers need a minimum order of $150 per delivery. Email wholesale@hearthandcrumb.example to ask about wholesale pricing.

## How do I talk to a person?

Our support assistant can answer most questions. If you need a person, ask the assistant to connect you, call us at (503) 555-0142, or email hello@hearthandcrumb.example. A team member replies to emails within one business day.
