# Allergens and Dietary Information

## Our kitchen

All of our products are made in one shared kitchen. We use these major allergens every day:

- Wheat (gluten)
- Milk
- Eggs
- Tree nuts (almonds, walnuts, hazelnuts, pecans)
- Sesame
- Soy

Our kitchen is peanut-free. We do not use peanuts or peanut products in any recipe, and we do not accept ingredients from suppliers that process peanuts in the same facility.

## Cross-contact

Because everything shares the same ovens, counters, and tools, any product can contain traces of wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts, sesame, or soy, even if the recipe does not include them. We clean our equipment carefully between batches, but we cannot guarantee that any product is free of these allergens. If you have a severe allergy, we recommend that you do not eat our products.

## Gluten-free options

We bake a small selection of gluten-free items, such as almond flour cookies and a buckwheat loaf. These are made without gluten ingredients, but they are baked in the same kitchen as our wheat products. They are not certified gluten-free and are not safe for people with celiac disease.

## Vegan options

These products contain no milk, eggs, butter, or honey:

- Country sourdough
- Seeded rye
- Baguette
- Oat and raisin cookies

All vegan products are made in the shared kitchen described above.

## Allergen labels

Every product in the shop has a card that lists its allergens. Every online product page also lists its allergens. For the allergens of a specific product, check its label, ask our support assistant, or ask a team member. If you are not sure whether a product is safe for you, contact us before you buy it.

## Recipe changes

We sometimes change recipes or suppliers. Always check the current label, even for a product you have bought before.
