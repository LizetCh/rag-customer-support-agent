# Hours and Location

## Address

Hearth & Crumb Bakery
1428 SE Alder Street
Portland, OR 97214

We are on the corner of SE Alder Street and SE 15th Avenue, across from Laurel Park.

## Regular hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday to Friday | 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM |
| Saturday | 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM |
| Sunday | 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM |

The shop is closed on Mondays so our bakers can rest and do a deep clean of the kitchen. Online orders placed on a Monday are processed on Tuesday morning.

## Holiday hours

We are closed on these days:

- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- New Year's Day (January 1)

On Christmas Eve (December 24) and New Year's Eve (December 31), we close early at 2:00 PM. Holiday pre-orders for pies and breads open on November 1 each year. Changes to our hours are posted on our website and on the shop door at least one week in advance.

## Parking and getting here

There is free street parking on SE Alder Street and SE 15th Avenue, limited to 2 hours. We do not have a private parking lot. Bus line 15 stops one block away at SE Belmont Street and SE 15th Avenue. There is a bike rack in front of the shop.

## Accessibility

The shop has a step-free entrance and a wheelchair-accessible restroom. If you need help carrying a large order to your car, ask any team member.

## Seating

We have 12 indoor seats and 3 outdoor tables. Seating is first come, first served. We do not take table reservations. Free Wi-Fi is available for customers.
