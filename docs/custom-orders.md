# Custom Cakes and Special Orders

## What we make

We make custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, and other events. We also take large orders of bread, pastries, or cookies for parties and offices.

## Cake sizes and prices

| Size | Serves | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| 6-inch round | 6 to 8 people | $45 |
| 8-inch round | 10 to 14 people | $65 |
| 10-inch round | 18 to 24 people | $90 |

Prices include one cake flavor, one filling, buttercream frosting, and a short written message. Fondant, fresh flowers, and custom toppers cost extra. We give you a final price before you pay your deposit.

Available flavors: vanilla bean, chocolate, lemon, carrot, and red velvet.

## How much notice do you need?

- Custom cakes: at least 72 hours (3 days) before pickup.
- Large orders of bread, pastries, or cookies (more than 4 dozen items): at least 48 hours before pickup.
- Tiered wedding cakes: at least 2 weeks before the event. We also offer a tasting appointment for $25, which is subtracted from the price of the wedding cake if you order it.

During the holiday season (November 15 to December 31), we need at least 1 week of notice for all custom cakes.

## How to order

Order a custom cake on our website, by email at hello@hearthandcrumb.example, or in the shop. We confirm every custom order by email within one business day.

## Deposit and cancellation

A 50% deposit is required to confirm a custom order. The remaining balance is due at pickup or before delivery.

- Cancel 7 or more days before pickup: full refund of the deposit.
- Cancel less than 7 days before pickup: the deposit is not refundable, but you can use it as store credit within 6 months.
- Changes to the design or size are free up to 72 hours before pickup.

## Pickup and delivery

Custom cakes are available for pickup at the shop or local delivery within Portland. Custom cakes cannot be shipped.
